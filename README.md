## How We Work

Our approach is grounded in research, experimentation, and engineering.

1. **Understand** — Study the problem, its context, and the people experiencing it.
2. **Research** — Explore existing solutions, technologies, and alternative approaches.
3. **Design** — Develop the architecture and define the right technical direction.
4. **Build** — Engineer the solution with attention to quality and reliability.
5. **Validate** — Test assumptions and measure real-world outcomes.
6. **Evolve** — Continuously improve, optimize, and expand.

We prefer building things that are useful, maintainable, and capable of evolving.
